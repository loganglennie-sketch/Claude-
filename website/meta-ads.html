FAQ

Common questions

How much should I spend on ads?

It depends on your area and what you sell. We'll suggest a starting budget on our first call and you can start small. Ad spend is paid directly to Meta.

Do I need to sign a long contract?

No. We start with a 30-day pilot at a fixed fee so you can see results before committing, then move to a simple monthly fee.

How quickly will I see enquiries?

Most campaigns start bringing in enquiries within the first couple of weeks, then improve as we learn what works.

Do I need to make the ads myself?

No. We write and design the ads. Photos or videos of your own work help a lot, and we'll tell you what's useful.
